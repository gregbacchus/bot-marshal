# ADR NNNN: Title in the imperative

* **Status:** `Proposed` | `Accepted` | `Superseded by ADR-MMMM` (with a link) | `Rejected`
* **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision. The constraints, the thing that would go wrong without one, and any
facts established by measurement or experiment rather than assumption. Write this so it stands
on its own — a reader a year from now has none of the surrounding conversation.

## Decision

What was decided, stated plainly and in the present tense.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table and why it lost. A decision with no rejected alternative usually
isn't one.

## Consequences

What this costs, what it makes harder, and what now depends on it. Be specific about the
downside — an ADR that lists only benefits is advocacy, not a record.
